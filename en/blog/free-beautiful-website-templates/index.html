Free beautiful website templates: 10 easy layouts

Free beautiful website templates in 10 layouts: classic, magazine, bento, minimal, dark, filtered, storytelling. How to pick the right one for your shop.

Visitors decide whether to stay on a website in the first few seconds. Free beautiful website templates help a small shop look professional from day one without hiring a designer. But “beautiful” is more than colours: the layout decides how quickly visitors find what they came for. Here are 10 layouts and how to choose the one that fits your shop.

Quick summary, a beautiful template should have:

  1. What makes a website beautiful
  2. Classic and magazine
  3. Bento and big-type minimal
  4. Elegant dark
  5. Filtered catalogue and app style
  6. Storytelling, split screen, one page one button
  7. Choose by how you sell

1. What makes a website beautiful

A beautiful website is easy to read and easy to tap: large enough text, generous white space, an accent colour only for key buttons, and real, sharp photos. On phones, the order or call button is always within thumb reach.

Web design has many schools of thought, but for a small shop remember one thing: each page has one main job, and visitors should see it straight away.

2. Classic and magazine

Classic (layout A): a big photo up top, sections stacked below. The easiest to read and fits any trade. See the gift shop template.

Magazine (layout B): a grid of big and small photos with serif headings. Great for shops with lots of good photos, like shoes or fashion. See the shoe store template.

3. Bento and big-type minimal

Bento (layout C): tiles of different sizes fitted together like a lunch box. Each tile holds one fact: price, hours, reviews. See the security camera template.

Big-type minimal (layout D): lots of white space, very large headings, few photos. Suits services that need clear prices and steps. See the home repair template.

4. Elegant dark

Dark (layout E): a black or midnight background, bright photos and thin type. For businesses that want a premium, trustworthy feel, such as finance, evening restaurants or a surf school. See the mortgage broker template.

This style needs great photos and little text per screen, or the page feels heavy.

5. Filtered catalogue and app style

Filtered catalogue (layout F): filters on the left, a dense product grid on the right. For shops with dozens to hundreds of items. See the optical shop template.

App style (layout G): a bottom tab bar, swipeable cards and big buttons. It feels like a familiar app. See the funeral services template.

6. Storytelling, split screen, one page one button

Step-by-step storytelling (layout H): scroll through chapters with a large photo alongside. For trades with a process, like swimming or driving lessons. See the swim school template.

Split screen (layout I): a fixed photo on one side and scrolling content on the other. See the sports store template.

One page, one big button (layout J): a short page with one main action, like requesting a quote. See the translation agency template.

7. Choose by how you sell

  • Many products: filtered catalogue or magazine.
  • Services with a price list: minimal or bento.
  • Classes or appointments: step-by-step storytelling.
  • A premium feel: dark.

Colours and fonts change in seconds, so pick the layout first. Open the free website template library, tap “Use template” and replace the content. The steps are in our guide to create a free website; online shops should also see free ecommerce website templates.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the colours of a free template?

Yes. Pick the main, background and accent colours in the Design tab and the site updates instantly. The layout stays the same, so pages stay balanced.

Are beautiful templates slow on phones?

No need to worry. Images are compressed and load as visitors scroll, and fonts are light web fonts. Every template is built for phones first.

Can I use a template for another trade?

Yes. The layout is a frame; change the text, photos and categories. The storytelling layout from a driving school works well for a swim school.

Does a beautiful site help with Google?

Good design keeps visitors longer, but Google mostly rewards clear content, speed and phone-friendliness. Every template ships with titles, descriptions and structured data.
