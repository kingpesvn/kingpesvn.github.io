Free wedding photographer website: dates and packages

Build a free wedding photographer website: real wedding galleries, hourly packages, a date check and a package builder with total and retainer.

Couples looking for a wedding photographer ask three things: do I love the photos, is our date open, and what’s the total? Answering each message one by one takes your evenings. This guide shows how to build a free wedding photographer website from a ready-made template, so couples browse your galleries, check their date and build a package before they ever email you.

Quick summary, 5 things to know:

  1. What couples look for on a wedding photographer’s site
  2. Real wedding galleries, filtered by style
  3. Hourly collections with clear prices
  4. Date check and package builder
  5. Destination weddings, inquiry form and contract

1. What do couples look for on a wedding photographer’s site?

They want to see a whole wedding day you shot, not just a few highlights. The wedding photographer template uses a split screen: a large portrait photo on the left that changes as you scroll, and text, collections and an inquiry button on the right. On phones, each section gets its own photo at the top.

Detail shots like rings, boutonnieres and invitations show couples you notice the small things. Photo: Birch Landing Home (CC0).

The sample studio is based in New York’s Hudson Valley, with an Italian version for couples marrying in Italy; change the name, prices and language to match your clients. Wedding photography is a field where style is what sets you apart, so your first screen should say clearly how you shoot.

2. Real wedding galleries, filtered by style

Each wedding is a card with a cover photo, the couple’s names, venue and month. Couples filter by style: barn, garden, city, Italy, beach. Clicking opens the album full screen, with arrow keys or swiping to browse.

Six to eight weddings with different light and venues are enough. Put the wedding that best matches your ideal client first, because the first card shows at double size.

3. Hourly collections with clear prices

Three collections list the hours, price and what’s included: number of photographers, image count, album and engagement session. The most booked collection gets its own label so couples choose faster. In the US, prices are shown before sales tax.

Photos of the reception help couples picture how many hours they need: ceremony, dinner, cake, dancing. Photo: Blissful plans123 (CC0).

Each collection has a “Choose this” button that preselects it in the package builder below.

4. Date check and package builder

A couple picks their wedding date and the site answers right away: open, on hold for another couple, or booked. Dates too soon or too far ahead get their own message. If it’s a weekday or the off-season, they also see the discount, for example 20% off a Wednesday.

The package builder adds the collection, extra hours, a second shooter, albums and travel, then takes off the date discount. The result is a total and a 30% retainer to hold the date. Sending the form drops these numbers straight into the inquiry.

Add-ons like an album, film photos or a rehearsal dinner session go straight into the total. Photo: Nbarth (CC0).

5. Destination weddings, inquiry form and contract

Travel fees are set by location: free locally, a few hundred miles away, anywhere in the country, or abroad. For weddings in Italy, the Italian version shows prices in euros automatically; see how to build a free multilingual website.

Destination weddings often book more than a year ahead; clear travel fees help couples decide faster. Photo: Manciniart (CC0).

The inquiry form asks for both names, the date, email, venue, guest count, collection and “how did you hear about us”. The approach section has 4 steps from the first call to album delivery, plus a sample wedding day timeline. If you also rent bridal gowns, see this guide to a wedding dress rental website.

Frequently asked questions

Should a wedding photographer website show prices?

Show a starting price for each collection. Couples usually shortlist photographers by budget before reaching out; with clear prices, the people who ask about your dates already fit your rates.

How do couples know if their date is open?

Enter your booked dates and dates on hold. The date check tells couples right away whether a date is open, on hold or booked, plus any weekday or off-season discount.

Will photos slow down my website?

The template converts photos to WebP, loads a size that fits the screen and only loads images as visitors scroll. Pick your best images; you don't need to put whole albums on the home page.

How do I quote destination weddings?

Add travel fees by location, for example local, within 200 miles or Italy. When a couple picks their venue, the travel fee is added to the total along with the retainer to hold the date.
