Build a website on your phone, no computer needed: 6 steps

How to build a website on your phone for a small shop in 6 steps: pick a template, edit the text, take product photos with the camera and publish for free.

You sell all day, there is no computer in the shop, but you still want customers to find you on Google? You can build a website on your phone, on the same phone you use to message customers. No app to install, no code, and the first version takes about 10–15 minutes. This guide goes step by step, with tips for working on a small screen.

Quick summary, 6 steps to build a website on your phone:

  1. Open the template library in your browser
  2. Pick a template for your trade and preview it
  3. Edit your shop name, text and colours
  4. Take product photos with the camera
  5. Add WhatsApp, phone and how to order
  6. Publish your website for free

1. Open the template library in your browser

Today’s smartphones are powerful enough to build a website, as long as the tool is designed for small screens. Open Chrome or Safari and go to the free website template library. No app download and no sign-up are needed at this stage.

Every step in this guide can be done one-handed on a phone. Photo: Dayne Topkin (CC0).

Use Wi-Fi the first time: previews and photo uploads will be faster.

2. Pick a template for your trade and preview it

Type your trade into the search box or filter by topic. Tap Preview to open the live version: this is exactly how customers will see the site on their phones. When you like one, tap Use template.

Two new templates this week: the tailor shop template filters styles by occasion and books fittings; the watch and shoe repair template has a “What is wrong with it?” helper so customers find the right service themselves. Both can be fully edited on a phone.

3. Edit your shop name, text and colours

On a phone the editor has two parts: the edit panel and the preview. The Desktop / Mobile switch in the middle of the top bar lets you check both screen sizes, even while holding your phone.

  • Content tab: shop name, tagline, introduction.
  • Design tab: main colour, background, logo.
  • Every change shows up in the preview straight away.

Typing long text on a phone gets tiring, so write your introduction in a notes app first and paste it in.

4. Take product photos with the camera

This is where a phone beats a computer. When you add a product or change a photo, tap Camera to open the rear camera directly. Photos are resized automatically so the site loads fast; nothing else to adjust.

Shoot inside the editor; no need to move photos to a computer. Photo: Gabriel Santiago (CC0).

Shoot near a window, on a plain background, holding the phone straight. See also product photography with your phone. A tailor can photograph each roll of fabric; a shoe repairer can show before-and-after shots so customers trust the work.

5. Add WhatsApp, phone and how to order

In the Contact tab, fill in your phone number, WhatsApp, address and opening hours. The map follows the address automatically. For tidier orders, turn on orders via Google Form: each order becomes a row in Google Sheets, which you can check on your phone.

6. Publish your website for free

Tap Publish, choose a name for the site and sign in with a free GitHub account. No account yet? Create one on the sign-in page, which also works on a phone. About 1–2 minutes later your site is online 24/7 with an HTTPS address.

Even a small stall deserves a website so customers can check prices before they come. Photo: Wikimedia Commons (CC0).

Once it is live, share the link on WhatsApp and social media and print a QR code for the counter.

What to watch out for on a phone

  • The draft lives in that phone’s browser. To switch devices, tap Download to save a .zip file, then open it with Open from file.
  • Turn the phone sideways when editing a long price list.
  • Don’t clear your browser data while a site is half done.

Not every tool can be edited on a phone. For example, Google’s help pages say Google Sites must be edited on a computer; see the Kingpes, Google Sites and Wix comparison to choose well.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an app to build a website on my phone?

No. Open Kingpes in the browser you already have, such as Chrome or Safari, pick a template and edit it right on the page.

Will an older phone work?

Yes, as long as the browser is still updated. Newer phones show the preview more smoothly, but editing text and photos works the same.

Can I lose my draft on the phone?

The draft is saved in that phone’s browser. To continue on another device, tap Download to save a .zip file, then open it there with Open from file.

Does publishing from a phone cost anything?

No. You need a free GitHub account; the web address and HTTPS are free. Only your own domain name costs a yearly fee.
