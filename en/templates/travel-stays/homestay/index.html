About this template

A free homestay website template for guesthouses, B&Bs, villas and small bungalows that want direct bookings instead of going through booking sites. It opens with a full-screen photo showing your starting price, rating and check-in time, then your rooms with guests, bed type, size, weeknight and weekend rates, each with a button that drops it into the booking form.

A 200+ photo album filters by rooms, spaces, breakfast and nearby, with a full-screen viewer. Guests pick check-in and check-out dates, the page counts the nights and estimates the total (Friday and Saturday at the weekend rate), then sends the request to your Google Sheets or by WhatsApp. There are also amenities, nearby sights, house rules, directions, a map and a second language with its own prices.

Frequently asked questions

Is this homestay website template free?

Yes. The template, editor and web address are free. You only pay if you want your own domain.

Do I pay commission on bookings from the website?

No. Guests send requests straight to you by WhatsApp, chat or Google Sheets, with no middleman.

Can weekend prices differ from weeknights?

Yes. Each room has a weeknight and a weekend rate, and the booking form uses the weekend rate for Friday and Saturday nights.
