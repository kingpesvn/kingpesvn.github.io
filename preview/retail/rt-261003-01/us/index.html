Frequently asked questions

How does 0% financing work?

Pay over 6–12 months with a partner credit card or a consumer-finance plan. We set it up in store in about 15 minutes.

Can I test ride first?

Yes, every model can be test ridden at the store. Book from the product page so we can have the right size ready and fully charged.

How do I choose a frame size?

Each product page lists the rider height for every size. At handover we adjust the saddle and bars to fit you.

How long do e-bike batteries last?

Lithium packs typically last 800–1,000 charge cycles. Charge at 20–30%, don’t leave it empty for long, and let it cool down after a hot ride before charging.

Do electric scooters need registration?

In most places, yes. Electric mopeds need to be registered, and we help with the paperwork when you buy.

Do you ship outside the city?

Yes. Bikes are assembled, tuned and boxed, then shipped by courier; we quote the cost before sending.
