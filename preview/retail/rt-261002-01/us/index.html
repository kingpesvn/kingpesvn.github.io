A small shop run by two sisters

Hoa Mộc began as a flower stall at Ho Thi Ky market in 2014. Every morning we hand-pick flowers at the wholesale market, arrange them to order and deliver across the city.

What customers say

Questions

How fast is delivery?

Within 2 hours in the city, 7 a.m.–9 p.m. Pre-orders can pick an exact time.

What does delivery cost?

Free over the minimum in the city; outer districts are charged by distance.

Will it look like the photo?

We send a photo before delivery. Seasonal flowers may be swapped for similar colours, and we always ask first.

Can you invoice a company?

Yes, VAT invoices for opening stands, sympathy flowers and company orders.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer, e-wallet, or cash on delivery.
