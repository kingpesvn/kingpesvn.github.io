Good to know

When do I need to order for same-day delivery?

Order by 3 pm for same-day delivery in the city. The product page closes slots that are too soon so we have time to wrap.

Is the price included in the gift?

No. We never put receipts or prices in the box; your receipt is sent to you separately.

Can I send a gift anonymously?

Yes. Tick "Keep my name secret" on the order form and your name will not appear on the card.

What if the recipient is not home?

We call before arriving. If we cannot reach them, we message you to rebook or leave it with reception.
