Good to know

When will my order arrive?

Order by 8 pm the day before for Tuesday, Thursday or Saturday morning delivery. The site shows your next delivery day.

Is the produce tested for pesticides?

Yes. We grow to VietGAP standards, keep a log for every harvest and run a quick residue test before shipping.

What if something arrives damaged?

Send a photo within 2 hours of delivery and we’ll replace or refund that item.

Can I get a weekly box?

Yes — write “weekly” in the order note and we’ll call to set a regular delivery day.
