Plant care basics

Six habits for happy plants

Every plant has its own care card. These rules work for all houseplants.

  • Water at the right time

    Push a finger 3 cm into the soil: if dry, water until it drains out; if damp, wait. Overwatering kills more plants than drought.

  • Read the light

    Low: over 2 m from a window. Bright indirect: by a window without sun on the leaves. Direct sun: balconies and sunny sills.

  • Free-draining soil

    Pots need drainage holes. Use gritty mix for succulents and coir-perlite mix for leafy plants.

  • Feed lightly

    In the growing season, use slow-release feed every 2 months. Pause in cool weather or right after repotting.

  • Repot when crowded

    Roots poking out or water rushing straight through mean it is time for a pot 2–4 cm larger.

  • Watch for pests

    Check under the leaves weekly. If you spot aphids or spider mites, wipe with mild soapy water and isolate the plant.

Good to know

How do I know a plant suits my space?

Use the "Which plant fits?" box at the top: choose the spot, the light and how much care you can give. Not sure? Send us a photo of the spot.

Where does the pet safety info come from?

We check each plant against the ASPCA list of plants toxic to cats and dogs. It is for reference only; if your pet chews a plant and seems unwell, see a vet.

Will the plant get damaged in transit?

The root ball is wrapped and the pot wedged upright in a ventilated box. If it arrives damaged, send a photo within 24 hours and we replace it within 7 days.

Do I need to repot straight away?

No. Let the plant settle for 1–2 weeks first. If you choose a pot when ordering, we pot it up for you.

What if the leaves turn yellow?

Usually too much water or too little light. Send us a photo of the plant and its spot; care advice is free for the plant’s lifetime.
