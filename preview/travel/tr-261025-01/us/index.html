Good to know

How much is the deposit and when is the balance due?

30% holds your seats; the rest is paid by bank transfer 7 days before departure.

How are children priced?

Children 5–11 pay 75% and share their parents’ bed. Under 5 travel free; 12 and over pay the adult price.

Can I join on my own?

Yes. Share a room with a guest of the same gender, or pick a single room with the supplement shown in the form.

What if the weather turns bad?

If boats can’t sail or a pass is closed, we change the plan or refund the services that couldn’t run.

What documents do I need for Thailand?

A passport valid for at least 6 months. Vietnamese citizens are visa-exempt for short stays; rules can change, so we check again before you go.
