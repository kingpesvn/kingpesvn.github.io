Your progress

From whitewater to green waves

  1. 01

    Whitewater

    Learn to lie, paddle and pop up on the sand, then ride whitewater to shore.

  2. 02

    Standing tall

    Keep your balance, look ahead and make gentle turns.

  3. 03

    Green waves

    Paddle out past the break and catch unbroken waves on your own.

  4. 04

    Reading the ocean

    Trim along the face, understand tides, currents and surf etiquette.

Good to know

Can I learn if I can’t swim?

You need to swim about 50 m. If not, message us for a private lesson in shallow water.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, water-resistant sunscreen and a bottle of water. Boards, rash guards and towels are provided.

What if conditions are bad?

We check the forecast every morning. If it isn’t safe, you can move your date for free or get a refund.

How old do kids need to be?

From 8 for the kids’ class. Children aged 6–7 can take a private lesson if they are comfortable in water.

How do I pay?

Pay at the beach by cash, bank transfer or card. No deposit needed.
