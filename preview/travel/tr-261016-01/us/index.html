Good to know

How does day-use pricing work?

The first 2 hours have a set price, then each extra hour is added. Over 6 hours, an overnight stay is better value.

Can you issue a company invoice?

Yes. Tick "I need a company invoice" and add the company name and tax ID; the e-invoice is emailed after check-out.

Is booking direct cheaper than apps?

Usually, because we pay no commission to a middleman. Prices on this page are direct rates.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Not on regular dates. At holidays and during the fireworks season we ask for a 30% deposit by bank transfer.

Is there car parking?

Free motorbike parking on site. Cars use a car park 100 m away; reception will show you.
