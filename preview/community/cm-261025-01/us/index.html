A loved one has just passed. What first?

There is no rush. Families only need to do the few things below; we guide you through the rest.

  1. Call our 24/7 line; our staff arrive within 1–2 hours
  2. If your loved one died at home, contact the nearest doctor or clinic for guidance
  3. Find the deceased’s ID documents
  4. Agree as a family on faith, cremation or burial
  5. Choose a portrait photo for the altar

Good to know

Will someone answer in the middle of the night?

Yes. The line is staffed 24/7 including holidays; we arrive within 1–2 hours in the city.

Will there be extra costs?

You get a written quote before we begin, and any extra is agreed with you first.

Can you follow our religious customs?

Yes. We arrange Buddhist, Catholic, Protestant and non-religious services and can invite clergy on request.

How do we pay?

A deposit when you sign, the balance after the service, by bank transfer or cash.

Can you bring our loved one home from another province?

Yes. Our dedicated vehicles bring your loved one home and we handle the transport papers.
