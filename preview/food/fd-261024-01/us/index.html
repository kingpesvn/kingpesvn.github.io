    Where the leaves grow

    Every region tastes different

    Altitude, climate and harvest season make the difference. Tap a region to see its teas.

    • Vietnam Tan Cuong, Thai Nguyen 100–300 m March – October Vietnam’s best-known green tea region: low hills, morning mist, small buds and a sweet aftertaste. 2 teas
    • Vietnam Tay Con Linh, Ha Giang 1.300–1.800 m March – September Centuries-old Shan Tuyet trees grow wild on high slopes, picked bud by bud by local Dao families. 3 teas
    • Vietnam Bao Loc, Lam Dong 900–1.500 m Year-round A cool plateau all year, known for rolled oolong and matcha. 4 teas
    • Vietnam Moc Chau, Son La 1.000–1.050 m April – November Rolling tea hills on a mild plateau, giving black tea its malty depth. 1 teas
    • Vietnam West Lake, Hanoi May – July (lotus season) Home of lotus-scented tea: each kilo takes over a thousand flowers, their stamens picked by hand at dawn. 1 teas
    • China Fujian 600–1.000 m April – August The home of jasmine tea: green tea scented again and again with night-blooming jasmine. 1 teas
    • China Yunnan 1.500–2.000 m March – October The cradle of pu-erh: large leaves fermented and pressed into cakes for ageing. 1 teas

    Brewing guide

    Brew it right

    Water too hot makes green tea bitter; steeping too long makes oolong harsh. Start here and adjust to taste.

    Tea Water Leaf Time Infusions
    Green tea80°C5 g / 150 ml45–60 s3–4
    White tea85°C4 g / 150 ml2 min4
    Oolong95°C7 g / 150 ml40–45 s6–8
    Black tea95°C4 g / 200 ml3 min2–3
    Pu-erhRinse once first100°C7 g / 150 ml15 s8–10
    Scented tea85°C4 g / 150 ml1 min3–4
    Herbal tea100°C3 g / 250 ml5 min1–2
    MatchaWhisk with a bamboo whisk75°C2 g / 70 ml30 s1

    Good to know

    How long does tea keep?

    Green and scented teas are best within 6–12 months, oolong and black tea within 1–2 years, and pu-erh mellows with age. Keep it dry, out of sunlight and away from strong smells.

    I’m new to tea. Where should I start?

    The three-tea set is the easiest start. For something gentle try oolong or jasmine; for something bold try black tea.

    Can you add our company logo to gift boxes?

    Yes, from 20 boxes. The logo goes on the card or is engraved on the wooden lid; please order 7 days ahead.

    Do you ship abroad?

    Yes. Tea is vacuum-sealed and teaware is wrapped in several layers. Shipping is quoted when we confirm your order.

    Will tea keep me awake at night?

    Green, oolong and black teas all contain caffeine. In the evening choose herbal teas like chrysanthemum, mint or hibiscus.
