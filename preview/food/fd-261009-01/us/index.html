Good to know

What's the minimum for office delivery?

Three plates or more ship free within 3 km. Smaller orders are delivered for a distance-based fee.

Do you offer monthly plans for companies?

Yes. Message us your headcount and delivery time and we'll send the weekly menu and a monthly price.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Tofu in tomato sauce and stir-fried greens every day, plus a vegetarian special on Wednesdays.
