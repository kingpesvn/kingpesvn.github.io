    Feature

    4 a.m. in a Ly Chinh Thang alley

    The bakery opened in 2009 with one brick oven and two sacks of flour. Every morning the dough is kneaded from 4 a.m. and left to rise long enough for a light crumb and a thin, crackling crust.

    The liver pâté is cooked the same day, the pork roll comes from the maker we have used since day one, and the pickles are made every other day. Nothing is left over from yesterday.

    Today we also bake for offices nearby: morning meeting trays, training-day lunches, small birthday parties. The bread still comes out of the old oven, just with a few more hands.

    Good banh mi is bread straight from the oven that crackles when you squeeze it.Co Ba, owner

    Good to know

    How far ahead should I order for the office?

    Under 50 portions: 2 hours ahead. From 50 portions, please order by 5 p.m. the day before so we can prepare the dough.

    Can you label each portion?

    Yes. Add the names and fillings in the notes, or send us a list by chat after ordering.

    Do you have vegetarian, mild or no-pâté options?

    There is a separate vegetarian banh mi made on its own board. For anything else, just note no chilli, no onion or no pâté.

    How do I pay?

    Cash or bank transfer on delivery. Company orders can pay by transfer after receiving the invoice.
