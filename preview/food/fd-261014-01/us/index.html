Directions

Good to know

Do you deliver?

Yes. Order here for our own delivery within 2 miles, or use the delivery apps. Pick-up orders skip the line.

Can I choose sugar and ice?

Yes. Every drink can be 100%, 70%, 50%, 30% or 0% sugar, with regular, less, separate or no ice.

Are the pearls made fresh?

We cook pearls every 3 hours and discard them after 4 so they stay soft and chewy.

Can I order for the office?

Sure. For 10+ drinks, call an hour ahead and we’ll deliver everything together.
