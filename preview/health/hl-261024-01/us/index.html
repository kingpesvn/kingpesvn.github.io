What is bothering your skin?

  1. 01Acne

    Blackheads, closed comedones, inflamed or hormonal acne on the chin. Squeezing often leaves marks and scars.

    See a doctor if

    • Red, painful or pus-filled spots
    • No improvement after 2–3 months of products
    • Spots leave marks or pits

  2. 02Melasma & dark spots

    Brown patches on the cheeks, sun spots and post-acne marks. Each type needs a different treatment.

    See a doctor if

    • Patches spreading or darkening
    • A spot changing shape or uneven in colour
    • You have used unregulated whitening creams

    • Pitted scars that bother you

  4. 04Ageing & fine lines

    Loose skin, lines around the eyes, dull dry skin. The method is chosen for your age and skin type.

    See a doctor if

    • You want to improve your skin but are unsure where to start
    • You are considering injections or laser

    Red, itchy or flaky skin and hives. The cause needs to be found before any medication.

    See a doctor if

    • Itching for more than a week
    • A fast-spreading rash, swollen lips or trouble breathing: go to emergency care now
    • Recurring eczema in young children

  6. 06Hair loss & nails

    Heavy shedding, thinning on top, patchy loss; thick or discoloured nails from fungus.

    See a doctor if

    • More than 100 hairs a day for weeks
    • Round bald patches
    • Nails discoloured, thick or painful

    Moles, skin tags and warts. Each one is checked to tell harmless spots from ones to watch.

    • A spot that bleeds, itches or ulcerates

Aftercare

  1. Apply SPF 30+ sunscreen every morning and reapply every 2–3 hours outdoors.
  2. After laser or a peel, no scrubbing, saunas or swimming for 5–7 days.
  3. Do not squeeze spots or pick scabs; let them fall off.
  4. Use only the medication and products your doctor prescribed.
  5. If redness, swelling or pain gets worse, or there is discharge, call us straight away.
  6. Keep your follow-up appointments so the plan can be adjusted.

Good to know

Do I have to buy a course on my first visit?

No. On your first visit you pay only the consultation fee. The doctor explains the plan and cost, and you decide later.

How soon does acne treatment work?

Usually after 4–8 weeks of regular treatment, depending on the type of acne and your skin. Follow-ups adjust the medication.

Does laser hurt, and will I need time off?

It stings a little and numbing cream is used when needed. Toning and RF need no downtime; fractional laser means 5–7 days of redness and light peeling.

Do you see children?

Yes. Children must come with a parent. Choose the doctor who sees children when you book.

How do I change or cancel?

Message or call at least 2 hours before and changes or cancellations are free.
