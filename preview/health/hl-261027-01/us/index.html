    Good to know

    Is the eye test free?

    Yes, about 20 minutes. It is a refraction for glasses, not a medical exam. For eye pain, redness or sudden blur, see an eye doctor.

    How soon are glasses ready?

    Common single-vision lenses are in stock, ready in about 2 hours. Thin, light-adaptive or progressive lenses take 3–5 days.

    Can I use a prescription from elsewhere?

    Yes. Bring it or send a photo by message. If it is over 12 months old, we suggest a free re-test.

    Are frames under warranty?

    12-month warranty on frame defects. Free adjustments, screws and nose pads for life.
