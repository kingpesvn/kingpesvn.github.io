    Good to know

    I do not know the part number. How do I search?

    Type a name (hammer, wood screw…) or a size (M8, 12 mm, 5 m) into the search. Or send a photo of the old part by chat and we will find it.

    How do bulk prices work?

    Each item gets 5% off from 10, 10% from 50 and 15% from 200. Screws and nails have their own tiers on the product page. Your quote calculates it.

    What does "Order in" mean?

    We do not keep that size on the shelf; it comes from the warehouse in 2–3 working days. Send a quote to reserve it.

    Can I get a company invoice?

    Yes. Tick "Company invoice needed" in the quote and add company details in the notes.

    What is the returns policy?

    Exchanges within 7 days for unused items with a receipt. Cut-to-size goods (cable, pipe, keys) cannot be returned.
