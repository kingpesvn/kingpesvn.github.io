Materials

Woods, boards and finishes

Each material has its own price and lifespan. We bring real samples to your home so you can touch and choose.

Solid oak

Beautiful grain, hard-wearing, lasts 20+ years. Great for tables, fronts and stairs.

Moisture-resistant MDF

Green moisture-resistant core at a fair price. Good for kitchens and bathrooms.

Walnut veneer

A thin layer of real wood on board: the look of solid wood, lighter and more stable.

Laminate & acrylic

Scratch-resistant, easy to clean, many colours. High-gloss acrylic for modern kitchens.

Stone worktops

Quartz, granite or marble. Heat-resistant, low-absorbing, many patterns.

Hardware & handles

Soft-close hinges and runners, brass handles, in-cabinet LED lights.

Frequently asked questions

Is the site visit free?

Free across HCMC, Binh Duong and Dong Nai. Elsewhere we charge travel, credited back if you sign.

How do deposits and payments work?

30% on signing, 40% when the joinery is finished at the workshop, 30% after handover.

How long does an apartment take?

A 70–100 m² flat usually takes 5–8 weeks from final design, depending on materials.

Can I order just a kitchen?

Yes. We make single pieces: kitchens, wardrobes, TV units and tables to your measurements.
