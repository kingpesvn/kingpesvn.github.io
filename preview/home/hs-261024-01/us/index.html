    What’s included

    Room by room

    The standard checklist for a deep clean. Add your own requests when booking.

    Living room

    • Vacuum and mop floors, corners and skirting
    • Dust shelves, cabinets, TV, lamps and ceiling fans
    • Wipe switches, sockets and door handles
    • Inside windows and sliding tracks within reach
    • Vacuum sofa, curtains and rugs

    Bedrooms

    • Change bed linen (if fresh sets are left out)
    • Wipe headboards, bedside tables and dressing table
    • Wipe wardrobe fronts and mirrors
    • Vacuum under the bed
    • Mop floors and empty bins

    Kitchen

    • Wipe worktops, tiles and sink
    • Degrease the hob and hood exterior
    • Wipe fridge, microwave and cabinet fronts
    • Wash up leftover dishes (hourly service)
    • Empty bins and mop the floor

    Bathroom

    • Scrub toilet, basin and shower
    • Descale glass screens and mirrors
    • Scrub grout, floor and corners
    • Wipe shelves, refill paper, deodorise drains
    • Empty bins

    Good to know

    Is the website price final?

    It is an estimate based on the area or quantities you enter. We confirm the price when we call back, after seeing photos or asking about the home.

    Do I need to provide supplies?

    No. We bring vacuums, mops, cloths and products. We only need power and water.

    How much do I save with regular cleaning?

    Weekly saves 15%, every two weeks 10% and monthly 5% on services marked "Recurring".

    What if I am not home?

    Leave keys with the concierge or let us in remotely. We send before-and-after photos by chat.

    How do I reschedule or cancel?

    Tell us 12 hours ahead and changes or cancellations are free.
