Good to know

Does Thai massage hurt?

Some stretches and pressure points feel intense, but it should not hurt. Choose your pressure when booking and ask your therapist to ease off at any time.

Should I choose 60 or 90 minutes?

60 minutes suits one area such as the neck and shoulders. For a full-body massage, 90 minutes is more relaxed.

Do I need to book?

Booking is best, especially evenings and weekends. The form only shows start times that finish before we close.

How do I change or cancel?

Message or call at least 3 hours before and changes or cancellations are free.

Can I request a female therapist?

Yes. Choose "Female" under Therapist when you book.
