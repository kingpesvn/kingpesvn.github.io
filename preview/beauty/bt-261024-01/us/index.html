03 Your appointment

From arrival to the mirror

A lash set takes about 90–150 minutes. You lie back and relax with your eyes closed.

  1. 1
    Consultation

    We look at your eye shape and habits, then choose style and length.

  2. 2
    Prep

    Lashes are cleansed and lower lashes covered. You close your eyes and relax.

  3. 3
    Application

    Each extension is placed 1 mm from the base, with no glue on the skin.

  4. 4
    Finish

    We brush through and check the result with you in the mirror.

  5. 5
    Refill reminder

    You get an aftercare plan and a refill reminder in 2–3 weeks.

04 Aftercare

Make it last longer

Follow these steps. If you see redness, swelling or strong itching, message us straight away.

  1. 0–24 hours

    Keep lashes dry, no steam, avoid rubbing your eyes.

  2. First 48 hours

    No swimming, no sleeping face-down, no eyelash curlers.

  3. Every day

    Brush lashes with a clean spoolie and use oil-free make-up remover.

  4. 2–3 weeks

    Refill when about 30–40% have shed to keep them even.

  5. Brows & lips: 7–10 days

    Light flaking is normal. Do not pick, use the balm as advised and avoid sun.

Good to know

Do extensions damage natural lashes?

Not when length and weight suit your lashes and they are removed with a proper remover. We choose the style to suit your natural lashes.

How long do extensions last?

Natural lashes shed in cycles, so a refill is needed after 2–3 weeks. Good aftercare helps.

Do brows hurt, and when is the colour final?

Numbing cream is used, so it only stings a little. Colour is dark at first and settles after about 4 weeks.

How should I prepare?

Come without eye make-up or curled lashes. For brows and lips, avoid alcohol and strong coffee for 24 hours.

How do I change or cancel?

Message us at least 4 hours before and it is free.
