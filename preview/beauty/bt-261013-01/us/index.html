Good to know

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking means no wait. Evenings and weekends are busy.

What if I need to change my time?

Message or call at least an hour before and we’ll move it, free of charge.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Kids under 10 have their own price, and we have a booster seat.

How long does coloring take?

About 1.5–2 hours depending on length. Bleaching or highlights take longer; we’ll tell you first.
