Usually two to three weeks, depending on your nails and daily routine. Removal is free when you get a new set.

Can I bring my own design photo?

Yes. Send it with your booking so we can quote and prep the colors.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking means no wait, especially evenings and weekends.
