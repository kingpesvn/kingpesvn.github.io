How a spa visit works

Every step is gentle, and you get photos by chat when we’re done.

  1. 1Check-in and coat check

    We check skin, ears and nails, and ask about your pet’s habits first.

  2. 2Gentle bath

    Shampoo matched to the coat, warm water, careful gland expression.

  3. 3Dry, trim and nails

    Layer-by-layer drying, a trim in the style you choose, smooth nails.

  4. 4Photos and pick-up

    We send photos when done and let you know when to collect.

Good to know

My pet is anxious and scared of the dryer.

We go slowly, take breaks and use a low dryer setting. We never use sedatives.

How long does a groom take?

A bath takes about an hour, a full trim 1.5–2 hours depending on size and coat. We message you when done.

Do you groom pregnant or senior pets?

Yes — just let us know so we can be extra gentle. Sick pets should see a vet first.

How does pick-up work?

Free within 2 miles for grooms over $40; further away we quote when confirming.
