How a visit works

Clear steps, and you know the cost before you decide.

  1. 01Book or walk in

    Book online to skip the wait. If your pet looks seriously unwell, call the emergency line.

  2. 02History and physical exam

    We ask about eating habits, weigh your pet, take the temperature and listen to heart and lungs.

  3. 03Price before treatment

    If tests or treatment are needed, we explain why and give you the cost first.

  4. 04Treatment and follow-up

    We message you before follow-ups and the next vaccine.

Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

Not required, but booking means less waiting. If your pet shows warning signs, call the emergency line for advice right away.

My pet has never been vaccinated. Where do we start?

Bring your pet in for a checkup first. The vet checks their health, deworms if needed and plans shots for their age.

How do I prepare for spay or neuter surgery?

We examine your pet and run a pre-op blood test, and tell you when to stop food when you book. Most pets go home the same day.

Can you help outside opening hours?

The emergency line is answered 24/7. The vet advises by phone and opens the clinic if your pet needs to be seen.
