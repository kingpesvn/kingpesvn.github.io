Good to know

Do I need to book a fitting?

Booking lets us prepare your codes and a private room. Walk-ins are fine, but weekends usually mean a wait.

How early should I book?

In wedding season (September to January) choose 4–6 weeks ahead so there is time to alter and hold your dress.

How much is the deposit?

30% of the rental holds the code, plus a refundable security amount at pick-up. Each item lists its amount.

What if the dress doesn't fit?

We do minor alterations free at the waist, hem and straps. Bigger changes are quoted first.
