Your path

4 levels, clear goals

Know where you are and what to work on next. Coaches check your skills before you move up.

  1. Level 1 · 4–6 weeks

    Starter

    Stance, footwork, basic strikes and a proper warm-up.

    • Guard and footwork
    • Basic straight and hook punches
    • Stretching and injury prevention

  2. Level 2 · 3 months

    Foundation

    Combinations, defence, pad and bag work.

    • 3–4 strike combinations
    • Slip, block and counter
    • Conditioning for 3 rounds

  3. Level 3 · 6 months

    Intermediate

    Controlled sparring, tactics and technique fixes.

    • Light sparring with gear
    • Reading attacks and distance
    • Grading test

  4. Level 4 · 12+ months

    Advanced & fight team

    Fight-camp training, regular sparring, amateur competition prep.

    • Full-round sparring
    • Personal conditioning plan
    • Amateur competition entry

Good to know

Can I join with no experience?

Yes. Choose a "Starter" or "All levels" class; the coach starts with stance and footwork.

What should I bring to the trial?

Sportswear, water and a towel. We lend gloves, wraps and gear.

How old do kids need to be?

Kids classes are for ages 6–12. Parents are welcome to watch the trial.

Is it easy to get injured?

Beginners do not spar. Controlled sparring with gear starts at Intermediate, always supervised. Tell your coach about any condition or old injury.

Can I pause my plan?

6-month plans can be paused for up to 30 days for illness or travel; just message us.
