Good to know

How do I pick running shoe size?

Measure your foot in the afternoon and allow about 1 cm so toes don’t hit the front on downhills. Use the size finder to convert.

Is there a fee to exchange sizes?

Free within 7 days if tags are on and the item hasn’t been used outdoors. We swap on delivery.

How much is delivery?

30,000 VND in the city, free over 500,000 VND. Other provinces take 2–4 days.

Do you sell to teams and clubs?

Yes. Orders of 10+ shirts or pairs get a discount and free name/number printing; message us for a quote.
