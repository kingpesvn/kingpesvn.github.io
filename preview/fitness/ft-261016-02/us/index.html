Frequently asked questions

No racket — can I still play?

Yes. Hire rackets and buy shuttles at the desk, or tick "Hire 2 rackets" when booking.

How do weekly bookings work?

Tick "Book the same slot every week" and pick the number of weeks. We keep that slot; a 30% deposit holds it.

Is there a fee to change or cancel?

Free with 3 hours’ notice. Later changes cost 50% of the booked time.

Do I need court shoes?

Yes, please wear non-marking indoor shoes to protect the mats. Shoes are available to hire.
