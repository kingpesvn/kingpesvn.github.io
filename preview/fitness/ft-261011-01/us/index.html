Good to know

I’m a beginner. Do I need a coach?

Your free trial includes a coach who shows you the machines and basic moves. After that, train on your own or book personal training.

Do I need to book group classes?

Yes, classes have limited spots. Tap “Book” in the timetable or message us.

Can I pause my membership when travelling?

6- and 12-month plans can be paused for up to 30 days. Just let the front desk know.

What should I bring?

Clean trainers, workout clothes and a water bottle. We provide towels, lockers and showers.
