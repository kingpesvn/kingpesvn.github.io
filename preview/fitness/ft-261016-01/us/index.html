Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Single bookings need no deposit. Weekly bookings and leagues pay 30% to hold the slot.

How do I cancel or change?

Tell us 3 hours ahead to move for free. Heavy rain? We reschedule.

When is peak time?

17:00–21:00 Monday to Friday. Weekends use the weekend price all day.

Can I book 90 minutes?

Yes. From 1 hour in 30-minute steps; if a price band changes mid-game, each part is charged at its own rate.
