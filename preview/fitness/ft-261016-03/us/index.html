Frequently asked questions

No racket — can I still play?

Yes. Tick racket or paddle hire when booking, or hire at the desk.

How is pickleball different from tennis?

The court is about a third of a tennis court, with solid paddles and a holed plastic ball, so it is easier to learn. Most beginners can play after one session.

How do memberships work?

Plans are monthly; unused hours do not roll over. Sign up at the desk or tap "Choose plan" and send the request.

What if it rains?

Courts are outdoors: if rain stops play, we move your slot for free or refund that hour.
