About Mây

A small wardrobe, chosen piece by piece

Mây started in 2019 as a vintage stall in Ben Thanh market. Today we choose our own fabrics, sew with a small workshop in Thu Duc and source handmade pieces from craft villages. Every item is tried on, washed and photographed for real before it hits the rail.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pick the right size?

Every product has a size guide. Add your height and weight in the notes and we'll call to advise before shipping.

What's the exchange policy?

Exchange size or style within 7 days with tags on and unwashed. If it's our mistake, shipping is free both ways.

Can I check the order before paying?

Yes. Open and try it on at delivery; if it's not right, hand it back at no cost.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes, 2–4 days nationwide; from 25,000 ₫, free over 500,000 ₫.

Do handmade pieces match the photos exactly?

Each handmade piece varies slightly in color and pattern. We'll send a photo of your actual item before shipping.
