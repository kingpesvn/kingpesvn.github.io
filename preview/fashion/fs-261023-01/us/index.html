Find your size from your foot length

Every country labels sizes differently. Measure once and the page converts to EU, US, UK and BR, and remembers it.

  1. 1Put a sheet of paper against a wall and stand on it, heel to the wall.
  2. 2Mark the tip of your longest toe and measure from the wall (cm).
  3. 3Measure both feet in the afternoon and use the longer one.
Enter a measurement, e.g. 24.5

How do I find my shoe size?

Measure your foot with the 3 steps in Find your size, enter the cm and the page shows EU, US, UK and BR sizes and saves it.

How do exchanges work?

Within 7 days, unworn with tags and box, exchange for free in store or by post.

Is the conversion right for every pair?

It is a guide; lasts can differ by half a size. Message us if unsure.
