Find your swim size

Swimwear fits close, so go by your measurements. Enter bust and hips to get a top size, a bottom size, saved for next time.

  1. 1Measure your bust around the fullest part, tape level.
  2. 2Measure your hips around the widest part.
  3. 3If the two give different sizes, take the larger one for one-pieces.

Wear longer, keep the colour

Keep your swimwear bright

  1. Rinse in fresh water

    Salt and chlorine stretch and fade fabric. Rinse in cool water as soon as you are out.

  2. Hand wash, don’t wring

    Use mild soap and press out the water, never twist.

  3. Dry in the shade

    Strong sun thins the fabric. Dry flat, never tumble dry.

  4. Rotate between suits

    Elastane needs about a day to recover. Two suits each last twice as long.

Good to know

How do I choose my swim size?

Measure bust and hips and use the size finder. If top and bottom differ, bikinis can be mixed; for one-pieces take the larger size.

Can I exchange swimwear sizes?

Yes, free within 7 days with tags and hygiene liner on. For hygiene reasons we can’t exchange items worn in the water.

Do you deliver to hotels?

Yes. Same-day in Nha Trang, just add your hotel and room. Other provinces take 2–4 days.

Is the fabric UV-protective?

Styles marked UPF 50+ block about 98% of UV rays. Still use sunscreen on exposed skin.
