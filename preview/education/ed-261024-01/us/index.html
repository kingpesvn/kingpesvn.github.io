Daily rhythm

A day at camp

A steady routine mixing activity and rest. Day campers join from 7:30 am to 5 pm.

  1. 06:30
    Wake up, morning stretch

    Lakeside warm-up, make your bed, wash up.

  2. 07:30
    Breakfast

    Set menu, with separate meals for allergies.

  3. 08:30
    Morning activity

    Forest hike, archery or ropes course by group.

  4. 11:30
    Lunch and quiet time

    Nap or read in the tent.

  5. 14:00
    Skills workshop

    First aid, knots, outdoor cooking.

  6. 16:00
    Paddling and swimming

    In a roped-off area with lifeguards.

  7. 19:30
    Campfire

    Songs, stories and a recap of the day.

  8. 21:00
    Call home, lights out

    Overnight campers call home for 10 minutes each night.

What they’ll do

Outdoor activities

  • Archery

    Light bows, rubber-tipped arrows, coached.

  • Kayaking

    Tandem kayaks on the lake, life jackets on.

  • Campfire nights

    Shows put on by each group.

  • Forest hikes

    3–6 km trails by age.

  • Ropes course

    Harness and double safety lines.

  • Stargazing

    Telescopes and star maps.

  • Team canoe

    Four-person canoes, learning teamwork.

  • Nature discovery

    Leaves, bugs and a forest notebook.

For parents

Safety comes first

Clear routines for every activity, from meals to bedtime.

  • One adult per six kids

    Each group keeps the same counsellor from first day to last.

  • Nurse on site 24/7

    On-site sick bay, district hospital 8 km away.

  • Insurance for every camper

    Accident cover for the whole session, included in the fee.

  • Water lifeguards

    Life jackets on, a roped swim zone and certified lifeguards.

  • Photos to parents nightly

    A private group chat per session with photos and tomorrow’s plan.

  • Allergy-aware meals

    Separate meals for allergies or vegetarians when told in advance.

Please list allergies, conditions and medicines when you sign up so the team can prepare. Camp does not replace your child’s own medical care.

Good to know

My child has never slept away. Is overnight OK?

Try a day camp or the Little campers’ day first. Overnight campers call home for 10 minutes each night and a counsellor sleeps near each group.

Can non-swimmers join?

Yes. Non-swimmers stay in the shallow roped area, always in a life jacket. Pick the right swim level on the form.

Is there transport?

A return coach from Hanoi is an add-on, with stops in Cau Giay and Ha Dong. You can also drop off at camp.

How do deposits and cancellations work?

A 30% deposit holds the place; the rest is due 7 days before camp. Cancel 14+ days ahead for a full deposit refund.

Can parents visit?

Seven-day sessions have a Family Afternoon on Saturday. Otherwise visits are limited so kids can settle in.
