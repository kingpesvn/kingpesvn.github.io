Good to know

Is the trial class free?

Yes. The trial class and placement test are completely free, with no obligation to enrol.

How old does my child need to be?

We teach children from age 6. Kids’ classes learn through games, songs and pictures.

Can I make up a missed class?

Yes. Let us know by chat and we’ll place you in another class at your level that week.

Can I pay monthly?

Kids’ courses can be paid monthly. Other courses are paid upfront, with discounts for early or group sign-ups.

Can you invoice my company?

Yes. We invoice companies and run in-house classes for groups of 6 or more.
