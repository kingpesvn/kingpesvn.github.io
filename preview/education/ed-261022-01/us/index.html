    Good to know

    I have never cooked. Can I join?

    Yes. Each class shows its level, and beginner classes start from knife skills and prep.

    What does the price include?

    Ingredients, tools, apron, recipes and the meal at class. The market visit is included in the Market & home cooking class.

    Can I reschedule or cancel?

    Rescheduling is free up to 24 hours before. Cancel 48 hours ahead to get your deposit back.

    Can kids join with their parents?

    The kids’ cookie class is for ages 6 to 12, each child with one adult.
