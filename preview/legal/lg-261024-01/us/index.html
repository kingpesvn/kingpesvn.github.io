Good to know

Do I need to send the originals?

A clear photo is enough for a quote. For certified translations, bring the original or a certified copy when you collect, so it can be checked.

How is a page counted?

About 300 words of source text. Short documents like birth certificates or IDs count as one page.

Will the translation definitely be accepted?

That is up to the receiving authority. Send us their requirements and we will follow the required format and advise if legalisation is needed.

When do I pay?

Under 20 pages, pay on collection. Larger projects pay a 50% deposit when the price is confirmed.

Do you work evenings and weekends?

Yes, with the Same-day option. Message us first so we can line up the right linguist.
