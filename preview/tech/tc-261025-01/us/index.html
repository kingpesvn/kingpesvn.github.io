Good to know

How many cameras does a home need?

Roughly one indoor camera per 35–45 m² plus one per entrance. Use the calculator and the technician fine-tunes it on site.

Do cameras keep recording without internet?

Yes. The recorder keeps saving to the hard drive; you just can't view remotely. Add a 4G backup router for continuous remote viewing.

How many days of recording are kept?

It depends on cameras, resolution and disk size. The calculator picks a drive big enough for the days you choose; it overwrites automatically when full.

Do I need permission to install cameras?

Cameras on your own property usually don't need a permit, but must not film other people's homes or private areas. Shops and offices should post a notice.
