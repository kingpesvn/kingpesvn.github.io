Frequently asked questions

Can non-members book meeting rooms?

Yes. Pick a room and time and send the request; reception confirms by message. Pay on arrival or by transfer.

Can I visit before signing up monthly?

Yes. Choose the plan you like and the time you want to come; reception will show you around and give you a free day pass.

Do you issue company invoices?

Yes. Tick the invoice box when booking and send your company details by message or email.

Can I book a room every week?

Yes. Tick “Book weekly” and choose the number of weeks; the estimate multiplies by the sessions.
