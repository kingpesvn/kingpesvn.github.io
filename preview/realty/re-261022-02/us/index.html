Good to know

How is the commission charged?

Buyers and renters pay nothing for viewings. Owners pay the agreed commission only when the deal is done.

Are the prices and photos accurate?

We take the photos at each home. Prices are the owner’s asking price when listed and may be negotiable; sold or rented homes are marked.

Can you help with a mortgage?

Yes. We introduce banks and help with the paperwork. The calculator is only an estimate; the bank gives the real figures.

What should I bring to a viewing?

Just book ahead. If you may pay a deposit, bring ID and ask about the title, management fees and handover date.
