Good to know

What does the rent include?

Rent includes furniture, Wi-Fi and weekly cleaning. Electricity, water, the service fee and parking are charged separately; see the estimator above.

Can I rent for less than 6 months?

Some apartments take 1–3 month stays at a higher price. Message us to see which are free.

What do foreign tenants need?

A valid passport and visa. We register your stay as soon as you move in.

Do viewings cost anything?

No. Book a time, visit or video call, and pay a deposit only if you like it.
