Suggested routes

Where to ride

01

Ha Giang Loop: Dong Van – Meo Vac

  • 350 km
  • 3 days
  • Moderate, many passes

The classic loop via Tham Ma slope, Lung Cu flag tower and Ma Pi Leng pass. Plan 3 days, 2 nights in Dong Van and Meo Vac.

02

Hoang Su Phi rice terraces

  • 220 km
  • 2 days
  • Easy to moderate

Best in the water season (May–June) and harvest (Sept–Oct). Narrow roads: a semi-automatic suits best.

03

Du Gia waterfall and Tay villages

  • 150 km
  • 2 days
  • Challenging, some dirt sections

Quiet and empty with dirt stretches after rain. Ride it only if you know manual bikes, or go with a driver.

Before you ride

Documents, deposit and tips

What to bring

  • Passport or ID card (original)
  • A valid motorbike licence of the right class for the engine size
  • A phone number we can reach during the trip

Deposit

  • 2,000,000 ₫ deposit in cash or transfer, refunded on return
  • Manual bikes: 5,000,000 ₫ deposit
  • Damage is charged from the parts price list posted in the shop

Mountain riding tips

  • Fill up in towns: mountain petrol stations are few and close early.
  • Slow down on pass bends and honk before blind corners.
  • Avoid riding after 5 pm in winter: fog rolls in fast.
  • Breakdown on the road: call us before using any other repair shop.

Documents and deposits follow our shop rules. Foreign riders need a licence that is valid in Vietnam.

Good to know

Can I rent without a licence?

We only hand bikes to riders with the right licence. Without one, book our local driver service.

Semi-automatic or scooter?

For the loop pick a semi-automatic: more climbing power and engine braking on descents. Scooters suit town and flat roads.

Can I return the bike elsewhere?

Yes, you can drop it in Hanoi and we ship it back by bus for 400,000 ₫. Tell us when booking.

How are rental days counted?

Each day is 24 hours from pick-up. Same-day return counts as one day. From 5 days a cheaper long-term rate applies.
