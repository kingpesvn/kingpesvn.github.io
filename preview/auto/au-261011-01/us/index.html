    Good to know

    Do you charge for an inspection?

    The general check is free. Computer diagnostics cost a small fee, deducted from the bill if you repair with us.

    How long does a service take?

    A minor service takes about an hour, a major one 3–4 hours. Book ahead to avoid waiting.

    What parts do you use?

    Genuine parts or quality aftermarket ones. The quote names each option so you can choose.

    Do you handle insurance claims?

    Yes. We take the photos, prepare the paperwork and deal with your insurer.

    Can you collect my car?

    Yes, within 10 km. Call or message us to arrange a pickup time.
