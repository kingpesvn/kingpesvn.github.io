How it works

Get your car in 3 steps

  1. 01

    Pick a car and send a request

    Choose dates, see the estimate and send. We message you to confirm.

  2. 02

    Pay a holding deposit

    Transfer 30% of the rental to hold the car. Full refund if you cancel 3 days ahead.

  3. 03

    Collect and check the car together

    Documents, photos of the car and a handover sheet: about 15 minutes.

What to bring

  • ID card or passport (original)
  • Valid car driving licence (class B or higher)
  • Driver aged 22+ with at least 1 year of experience

Deposit

  • 5,000,000 ₫ deposit in cash or transfer, refunded on return
  • Or leave a motorbike with its own registration papers
  • Premium 7-seaters and EVs: 10,000,000 ₫ deposit

Documents and deposits follow our shop rules and may vary by car. Ask first if you are a foreign visitor.

Rental terms

Clear before you drive off

  • 300 km per day

    Extra km cost 3,000 ₫ each. Unlimited km on rentals of 7 days or more.

  • Same fuel level back

    Return with the fuel you received. EVs back with at least 30% charge.

  • Late returns

    Under 1 hour is free, then 100,000 ₫/hour; over 5 hours counts as a day.

  • If there is damage

    Insurance pays under the contract; your excess is at most 3,000,000 ₫ per incident.

Good to know

What documents do I need?

An original ID card or passport and a valid class B licence, plus a 5 million dong deposit or a motorbike with its registration. These are our shop rules.

How is a rental day counted?

Each day is 24 hours from pick-up. Same-day pick-up and return counts as one day. Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays use the weekend rate.

Are longer rentals cheaper?

Yes. From 7 days every day uses the long-term rate with unlimited km. Message us for monthly quotes.

Can I drive to other provinces?

Yes, just tell us your route. For EVs pick routes with chargers; we send you a list.
