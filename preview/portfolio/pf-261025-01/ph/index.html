01 Story

From a tiny rehearsal room to the big stage

Gió Mùa started in a 12 m² rehearsal room on Hang Bac Street and has since played more than 240 stages across Vietnam and Southeast Asia.

  1. 2018
    The first rehearsal

    Four university friends rent a room by the hour and play covers at weekends.

  2. 2020
    Debut single “Mưa Tháng Sáu”

    Recorded at home during lockdown and unexpectedly shared far and wide.

  3. 2022
    A saxophonist joins

    The sound picks up a jazz colour and the band starts touring the provinces.

  4. 2024
    Album “Phố Cũ”

    Ten songs about Hanoi, launched with 12 sold-out nights.

  5. 2026
    EP “Đêm Không Ngủ” and a Southeast Asia tour

    First shows in Manila and Cebu.

Press kit

For press and promoters

Gió Mùa is a five-piece indie pop band from Hanoi, formed in 2018. Their music blends electric guitar, saxophone and an unpolished voice, telling stories of old streets and long nights.

The band has released one album, one EP and several singles, playing festivals, lounges and events across Vietnam and the Philippines.

Good to know

How far ahead should we book?

Ideally 3–4 weeks ahead, earlier for wedding season (September to December). Short notice is possible if we are free.

Do you bring your own sound?

Acoustic and full band packages use the venue’s sound. The production package brings full sound, lighting and a technician.

Do you take song requests?

Yes. Send your list when confirming; we learn up to 3 extra covers at no charge.

How does the deposit work?

30% deposit on signing, the rest after the show. Cancel 14+ days ahead for a full refund of the deposit.
