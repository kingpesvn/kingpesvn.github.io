Frequently asked questions

Is the price per person or per room?

Per room, per hour. Pick a room for your group size; the price stays the same even with fewer people.

What if we stay longer?

If the room is free, we charge by the half hour. Tell reception 15 minutes ahead to keep the room.

Can we bring our own cake?

Birthday cakes are welcome. Other food and drinks must be ordered here.

Do kids need an adult?

Guests under 16 must come with an adult, especially after 9 pm.
